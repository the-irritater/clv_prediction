## 🎯 Problem Statement

E-commerce companies struggle to allocate marketing budgets efficiently. By predicting **Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)**, businesses can:

- **Focus retention spend** on high-value customers (~80/20 rule)
- **Identify at-risk segments** before churn occurs
- **Forecast future revenue** for financial planning

> A 5% improvement in retention of top-decile customers can increase profits by **25–95%** (Harvard Business Review).

**Target Variable:** `clv` — Total revenue generated by a customer over their lifetime.

## 📊 Approach

### Data
- **50,000 customers** and **~350K+ transactions** over 3 years
- Realistic patterns: Pareto spending, seasonal spikes, churn simulation, age-correlated preferences

### Feature Engineering (20+ features)
| Category | Features |
|----------|----------|
| **RFM Core** | recency, frequency, monetary |
| **Order Stats** | avg/max/min order value, order std, total quantity |
| **Temporal** | tenure, customer lifespan, inter-purchase time (mean/std) |
| **Behavioral** | category diversity, weekend ratio, preferred month, purchase velocity |
| **Engagement** | discount sensitivity, rolling 3m/6m revenue, churn flag |

### Models
| Model | Type | Tuning |
|-------|------|--------|
| Ridge Regression | Baseline | — |
| Random Forest | Intermediate | Grid |
| XGBoost | Advanced | Optuna (30 trials) |
| LightGBM | Advanced | Optuna (30 trials) |

### Evaluation Metrics
- **RMSE** — Root Mean Squared Error
- **MAE** — Mean Absolute Error
- **MAPE** — Mean Absolute Percentage Error
- **R²** — Coefficient of Determination
- **5-Fold Cross-Validation** on best model

- **Local:** Waterfall plots for individual high/low CLV predictions

## 🎯 Customer Segmentation

K-Means clustering on standardized RFM features with silhouette-based k selection:

| Segment | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Champions | High frequency, high monetary, recent |
| Loyal Customers | Consistent buyers with good CLV |
| At-Risk | Previously active, declining engagement |
| Lost / Hibernating | Long inactive, low recent activity |

| Check | Frequency | Threshold |
|-------|-----------|-----------|

## 📈 Business Insights

1. **Top 10% of customers generate ~60% of revenue** — focus retention efforts here
2. **Referral channel produces highest-value customers** — increase referral incentives
3. **Customers who buy from 3+ categories have 2x higher CLV** — cross-sell aggressively
4. **Recency is the strongest predictor** — re-engage customers before the 90-day churn threshold
5. **Holiday season (Nov-Dec) drives 35% of annual revenue** — plan inventory and campaigns accordingly
